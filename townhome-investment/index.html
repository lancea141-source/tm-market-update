Part 1 — This Month's Read

For Buyers & Investors

Herriman currently offers the most favorable entry point among target cities — resale townhomes running 16.5% below the Salt Lake County median $/sqft. For Sellers

Salem is moving fastest among target cities at a median 32 days on market — sellers there are positioned to price with confidence. Herriman’s resale pricing sits below its county median, which is worth knowing before listing there — buyers are comparing against that baseline.
